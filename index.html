Room sizes are the clear sizes from the sale deed plan (22.8.23). Wall thickness, doors and windows are scaled from the drawing, so they are close but not surveyed.

  • LivingKeep the wooden wall as the TV wall. Put a 3-seater sofa facing it about 2.5 m away, and keep a 0.9 m path clear to the balcony door.
  • DiningA 4-seater table, about 1.4 × 0.9 m, fits in the centre of the dining hall and still leaves walking space to the kitchen, both bedrooms and the washroom.
  • EntryUse the C.B. niche beside the main door as a closed shoe and crockery cabinet, so shoes never stay in the corridor.
  • Bedroom 1Bed headboard on the right-hand wall. That leaves the balcony door and the wardrobe doors free to open fully.
  • Bedroom 2The small lobby beside the attached washroom suits a dressing table or a compact study desk with a mirror.
  • FinishWarm-white LED ceiling lights, light curtains and two balcony planters make the flat look ready to move in at low cost.
  • WashroomsAdd a mirror cabinet over each basin and a small exhaust fan in both washrooms, to keep them fresh and dry.

"Furniture ideas" shows this furniture in the model. It is a suggestion only and is not part of the flat as it stands today.
